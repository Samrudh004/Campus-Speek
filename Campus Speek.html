Core Capabilities

Built for Fairness
Designed for Excellence

CAMPUS-SPEEK is a next-generation complaint management platform engineered to bring transparency, accountability, and swift resolution to every academic institution.

🔒
Anonymous Filing
Submit complaints with full anonymity. Your identity is protected by military-grade encryption protocols.
⚡
Real-Time Tracking
Monitor your complaint status live with instant notifications at every milestone of the resolution journey.
📊
AI-Powered Triage
Intelligent categorization automatically routes each complaint to the appropriate authority for swift action.
🏛
Multi-Department Routing

Complaints are intelligently routed to the right department — academics, facilities, administration, welfare, or finance — ensuring the most qualified person handles your case.

📋
Structured Escalation

A tiered escalation protocol ensures unresolved cases are automatically elevated through the chain of command, up to the institution's highest authority.

🔔
Smart Notifications

Receive elegant, real-time notifications via email or in-app alerts at every stage — from acknowledgement to final resolution and closure.

📁
Evidence Management

Attach documents, images, and recordings as supporting evidence. Our system maintains a tamper-proof chain of custody for all submitted materials.

🌐
Public Transparency Board

An anonymised public board displays campus-wide complaint trends, fostering accountability and demonstrating institutional commitment to resolution.

📈
Analytics & Insights

Administrators gain access to powerful dashboards revealing complaint patterns, resolution velocity, and departmental performance metrics.
